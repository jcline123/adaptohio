Adapt Ohio

Resources, experiences, and ideas for families of children with limb differences and disabilities

Why we made this

We're the parents of Gwendolyn, who was born with fibular hemimelia and apodia. She had amputation surgeries at 9 months old and is now a bilateral below-knee amputee who walks, runs, plays, and keeps us busy.

Adapt Ohio started as a place for us to keep track of things we found along the way: programs Gwen has tried, places our family enjoys, resources that helped, and ideas we want to remember for later. Some are useful now. Others are here for when Gwen is older or when the right time comes.

Our experience is centered on limb differences, but many of the programs, places, and resources here may be helpful to families of children with other disabilities as well. We thought sharing this information might be useful to other families. We're not experts, and this isn't a step-by-step plan. It's simply one family's growing collection.
