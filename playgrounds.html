Accessible Playgrounds

← Back to Home

Central Ohio has more accessible and inclusive playgrounds than we originally realized. Some have fully ramped structures and smooth rubber surfaces, while others have a smaller number of adaptive features. We organized them by area to make it easier to find places close to home or plan a playground trip somewhere new.

Accessibility can mean different things at different parks. If your child needs a specific surface, swing, restroom setup, or wheelchair-accessible play feature, it is worth checking the park information before making a special trip.

Columbus

Carriage Place Park Accessible Playground

Address: 4900 Sawmill Road, Columbus, OH 43235

Open in Google Maps

Carriage Place is one of Columbus Recreation and Parks' universally accessible playgrounds. Parts of the play area use poured rubber, which can be easier for prosthetics and mobility devices, while other portions use engineered wood fiber.

Features: Universally accessible design and poured-rubber play areas.

Franklin Park Accessible Playground

Address: 1755 E Broad Street, Columbus, OH 43203

Open in Google Maps

The playground at Franklin Park has smooth rubber paths leading through the play area, ramps into the structure, accessible swings, and outdoor musical equipment. It offers a nice mix of active play and things children can use from ground level.

Features: Rubber pathways, ramps, accessible swings, and musical play.

Livingston Park Accessible Playground

Address: 760 E Livingston Avenue, Columbus, OH 43205

Open in Google Maps

Livingston Park is another of the city's universally accessible playgrounds. One of its standout pieces is a large platform-style glider that allows children with different mobility needs to play together.

Features: Wheelchair-accessible play and a shared platform glider.

Rhodes Park Accessible Playground

Address: 1910 W Broad Street, Columbus, OH 43223

Open in Google Maps

Rhodes Park combines a universally accessible playground with additional adaptive recreation space. The nearby Ken Campbell ADA Sports Pad and accessible playing field make this a useful option for families looking for more than a traditional playground.

Features: Universally accessible playground, ADA sports pad, and accessible field space.

Sullivant Gardens Park Accessible Playground

Address: 755 Renick Street, Columbus, OH 43223

Open in Google Maps

Sullivant Gardens is listed by Columbus as a universally accessible playground. Detailed information about its individual equipment and surfacing is limited, so families looking for a particular feature may want to check current conditions first.

Features: City-designated universally accessible playground.

Warner Woods All-Abilities Playground

Address: 4790 Warner Road, Columbus, OH 43081

Open in Google Maps

Warner Woods has a poured-rubber surface, ground-level play pieces, and a wheelchair-accessible carousel. The smooth paths, accessible seating, and equipment that can be used without climbing make it one of the stronger all-abilities options in the area.

Features: Rubber surface, wheelchair-accessible carousel, and ground-level play.

Whetstone Park Accessible Playground

Address: 3923 N High Street, Columbus, OH 43214

Open in Google Maps

Whetstone Park is included on Columbus' list of universally accessible playgrounds. The park also has plenty of open space and paved routes, though detailed information about the playground's individual adaptive features is limited.

Features: City-designated universally accessible playground.

Westerville and Northeast Columbus

Blendon Woods Inclusive Playground

Address: 4265 E Dublin Granville Road, Columbus, OH 43081

Open in Google Maps View park information

This is one of the largest and most accessible playgrounds in the area. It has ramps, communication boards, sensory and musical play, accessible seating, and a fully fenced double-gated play area. Gwen loves having so much room to run and play without barriers, and it has become one of our go-to spots.

Features: Ramps, smooth routes, sensory play, communication boards, fencing, and a companion-care restroom with a universal changing table and hoist.

Hours: Public playground hours are limited because some times are reserved for families and groups serving people with disabilities. Check the Metro Parks page before visiting.

Inclusive playground at Blendon Woods Metro Park

Millstone Creek Inclusive Boundless Playground

Address: 745 N Spring Road, Westerville, OH 43082

Open in Google Maps

Millstone Creek's Boundless Playground was designed for children ages 2 through 12 and includes accessible restrooms, a family restroom, changing stations, pathways, swings, and nature play.

Features: Boundless play design, accessible facilities, changing stations, pathways, swings, and nature play.

Temporarily closed: Westerville's latest project update moved the expected reopening to August 2026. Check with the city before planning a visit.

Dublin

Miracle League Playground at Darree Fields

Address: 6259 Cosgray Road, Dublin, OH 43016

Open in Google Maps View park information

This playground is beside the Miracle League Athletic Field and has accessible routes that make it easier for kids with prosthetics or mobility devices to get around and join in. The accessible ballfield, parking, and restroom complex make the whole area especially useful for adaptive recreation.

Features: Accessible playground beside a specially designed Miracle League ballfield.

Darree Fields offers shaded play and peaceful moments, apple included.

Emerald Fields Accessible Playground

Address: 4040 Wyandotte Woods Boulevard, Dublin, OH 43016

Open in Google Maps

The existing playground at Emerald Fields has rubber surfacing, a wide open layout, and a separate area for younger children. The smooth surface can be especially helpful for children using prosthetics, walkers, or wheelchairs.

Features: Rubber surfacing, open circulation, and separate play space for younger children.

Hilliard

Homestead Metro Park Accessible Play Areas

Address: 4675 Cosgray Road, Hilliard, OH 43026

Open in Google Maps

Homestead Metro Park has two wheelchair-accessible play areas along its paved park routes. Sand and sensory tables give children more options for ground-level play, even if they are not interested in climbing through a larger structure.

Features: Two accessible play areas, paved routes, and sand and sensory tables.

Hilliard East Park Playground

Address: 4450 Schirtzinger Road, Hilliard, OH 43026

Open in Google Maps

Hilliard East is not described as a fully inclusive playground, but it does have an adaptive swing. It can be a good neighborhood option for a child who benefits from that specific feature.

Features: Adaptive swing and nearby seasonal restrooms at the pool.

Worthington, Powell, and Orange Township

All Children's Playground at McCord Park

Address: 345 E Wilson Bridge Road, Worthington, OH 43085

Open in Google Maps

Located beside the Worthington Community Center, the All Children's Playground has rubber surfacing and a ramped play structure. Those two features make it easier to move through the playground with prosthetics, a walker, or a wheelchair.

Features: Ramped structure and rubber play surface.

Every Kid's Playground at Liberty Park

Address: 2845 Home Road, Powell, OH 43065

Open in Google Maps

One of Liberty Park's three playgrounds is fully accessible. The equipment includes several pieces children can use from a seated position, along with a family restroom nearby.

Features: Rolling slide, shared glider, inclusive seesaw, wheelchair-height activity tables, sand play, and family restroom.

North Orange Park Accessible Playground

Address: 7560 Gooding Boulevard, Delaware, OH 43015

Open in Google Maps

North Orange Park is included in local accessibility listings for its ADA-oriented playground features. The available descriptions do not clearly identify the surface or individual equipment, so it is worth checking ahead if your child needs something specific.

Features: Multiple accessible playground elements within a larger township park.

Gahanna

Sunpoint Park Inclusive Playground

Address: 670 McCutcheon Road, Gahanna, OH 43230

Open in Google Maps

Sunpoint Park was designed for a wide range of ages and abilities. It has a strong mix of accessible routes, sensory play, movement, and traditional playground activities.

Features: Adaptive swings, sensory areas, wheelchair-friendly paths, inclusive structures, accessible-height basketball hoops, and heated restrooms.

Upper McCorkle Park Inclusive Playground

Location: Near 200 McCutcheon Road, Gahanna, OH 43230

Open in Google Maps

Gahanna rebuilt this playground in 2024 with a more accessible and inclusive layout. Paved connections and nearby parking make it easier to reach, though detailed information about the individual play pieces is limited.

Features: Rebuilt inclusive playground with paved access and nearby parking.

Grove City

Gantz Park All-Accessible Playground

Address: 2255 Home Road, Grove City, OH 43123

Open in Google Maps

Gantz Park has inclusive playground equipment designed for children with different abilities. The nearby Garden of the Five Senses adds another place to explore sound, touch, color, and scent.

Features: All-accessible playground equipment and a nearby sensory garden.

Dream Field Playground at Windsor Park

Address: Near 3400 Ventura Boulevard, Grove City, OH 43123

Open in Google Maps

The Dream Field Playground is an accessible, boundary-free play space beside Grove City's inclusive Dream Field. This is another good option for families who want a playground and adaptive sports space in the same visit.

Features: Boundary-free sensory play and an adjacent rubberized adaptive ballfield.

Discovery Frontier at Fryer Park

Address: 3899 Orders Road, Grove City, OH 43123

Open in Google Maps

Discovery Frontier is a space-themed playground designed for children of different abilities. Fryer Park also has restrooms, picnic areas, a splash pad, and other activities nearby, making it easy to spend more time there.

Features: Fully accessible play structure, restrooms, nearby splash pad, and nature play.

New Albany

Taylor Farm Park Playground

Address: 5526 E Dublin-Granville Road, New Albany, OH 43054

Open in Google Maps

Taylor Farm has a rubberized surface that keeps the playground easier to navigate for children using prosthetics or wheeled mobility devices. Separate areas for younger and older children help make the space feel more manageable.

Features: Rubber surface, separate age areas, accessible parking, restrooms, and shade structures.

Sumption Park Playground

Location: The Links neighborhood, New Albany, OH

Open in Google Maps

Sumption Park has newer playground equipment, an accessible play surface, seating, and shade. The exact mix of adaptive equipment is not clearly listed, but the smoother surface makes it worth including for families looking for easier ground access.

Features: Accessible play surface, newer equipment, seating, and shade.

Upper Arlington

Fancyburg Park

Address: 3375 Kioka Avenue, Upper Arlington, OH 43221

Open in Google Maps View park information

Fancyburg has an accessible play structure, plenty of open space, and several other activities nearby. It has been a good place for us to meet friends and spend time outside.

Features: Accessible play structure, open park space, shelter, and nearby sports areas.

Gwen playing at Fancyburg Park in Upper Arlington.

PRIDE Playground at Greensview Elementary

Address: 4301 Greensview Drive, Upper Arlington, OH 43220

Open in Google Maps

The PRIDE Playground has a wide mix of seated, sensory, spinning, balance, and musical activities. Because it is on school property, public use is generally limited to times when school activities are not taking place.

Features: Accessible-seat swing, activity table, outdoor instruments, spinners, and balance equipment.

School playground: Plan visits outside school hours and follow any posted campus rules.

Welcome Playground at Barrington Elementary

Address: 1780 Barrington Road, Upper Arlington, OH 43221

Open in Google Maps

The Welcome Playground has a ramped structure, accessible swings, a shared glider, ground-level play, and a quieter retreat space. Like the Greensview playground, it is generally available to the public only outside school-use hours.

Features: Ramped structure, accessible swings, inclusive glider, sensory play, and retreat space.

School playground: Plan visits outside school hours and follow any posted campus rules.
